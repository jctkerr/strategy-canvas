# Work through a question

**Could our bookshop run a workshop without losing money?**

This fictional example shows how a broad question becomes a calculation and a useful next step.

[Open the editable tree](https://jctkerr.github.io/strategy-canvas/bookshop.html)

![A workshop question branches into demand, delivery and break-even attendance. The cost branch divides £120 by £15 per ticket to get eight paying attendees.](images/bookshop-tree.png)

## 1. Start with your question

After [setup](../README.md#with-your-ai-tool), tell your agent:

> Let's use a fictional bookshop. Could one workshop cover its costs? Tickets are £20, materials £5 per person and fixed costs £120. We have five staff-hours. Demand and room capacity are unknown. Help me think it through in the canvas.

## 2. Break it into smaller questions

Look at **demand**, **delivery** and **costs**. This is an **issue tree**: each branch helps answer the question above it.

The cost branch uses a **driver tree** to show the calculation. Different branches can use different tree types.

## 3. Work one branch

Each £20 ticket leaves **£15** after £5 of materials. The workshop needs **8 paying attendees** to cover £120 of fixed costs:

**£120 ÷ (£20 − £5) = 8**

Eight covers the stated costs. It does not tell us whether eight people will book, or whether we can run the event as costed. The useful next question is: **what booking evidence do we have?**

## 4. Change something

Tell your agent:

> Materials now cost £8 per person. Update the calculation and any questions affected. Keep the other branches.

| | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Money left from each £20 ticket | £15 | £12 |
| Paying attendees needed | 8 | **10** |

The demand question becomes **“Would 10 people buy a £20 ticket?”** Staffing and booking evidence stay unresolved.

[See the updated tree](https://jctkerr.github.io/strategy-canvas/bookshop-updated.html)

You can also click a card to edit, then **Save**; use **+** to add a branch. Card text does not recalculate itself. Update linked thoughts yourself or ask your agent. In the browser example, **Export → Interactive canvas** saves a copy; check that it downloaded.

## Why these trees?

Issue trees help break down a problem; driver trees make a number's calculation explicit. See [the tree guides](../references/tree-methods.md) and their sources: [McKinsey on structured problem solving](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-to-master-the-seven-step-problem-solving-process), [Bain's break-even case](https://www.bain.com/careers/hiring-process/interviewing/coffee-case-study/).

The bookshop and figures are invented for teaching. This is an initial viability inquiry, not a complete event-launch plan.
