How to use
Work through a problem, one branch at a time.
Your conversation stays in your agent’s chat. This canvas keeps the questions, possibilities and evidence in view as you think together.

 
  1. Start with the question. The first card is your starting point: a question, goal or claim. Read its branches from left to right. Use Problem to clarify what you want to change and the limits that matter.
  2. Develop one thought. Click a card to edit it, or use its + to add a child. Give it a short label, put the reasoning in notes, then save. A new draft adds nothing until you save it.
  3. Check what you know. Record evidence and its source. Keep assumptions and unknowns visible. Ask your agent what else could explain the situation, or what would change the decision.

Follow the connections
A line links a thought to the card on its left. Its label explains the relationship: an input to a calculation, a possible explanation, a supporting reason or another kind of connection. Tree type on the selected card opens the choices; your agent can choose with you.

 
Choose a tree, or combine several
Start with what you need: break down a problem, test an explanation, understand a number or compare a choice. Tree type shows the options, with examples tucked under Example & help. Each subtree can use a different method; set it on that subtree’s first card. A method label on the card shows where it starts.

 
Keep the view manageable
Quick start expands or collapses the introduction. The small chevron on a card reveals or hides its children. Focus branch shows just the selected branch; Whole tree brings you back. Drag the background to pan, use the zoom controls, or choose Reset view.

 
Understand the status
Open means still being explored. Uncertain marks a gap or assumption. Supported is a recorded judgement; inspect its basis in Details. The canvas does not verify claims for you. Undo reverses your most recent saved edit until another editor updates the session.

 
Keep your work
Export includes all branches, notes and sources. Review them before sharing. The question at the bottom is a suggestion to discuss in your agent’s chat.
